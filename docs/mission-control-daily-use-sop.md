# Mission Control Daily Use SOP

This is the practical day-to-day guide for using Mission Control as the main place for ClinicGrower leads, follow-ups, proposals and client onboarding.

The goal is not to create more admin for the team. The goal is to stop important information being split across Scoro, email, WhatsApp, notes and memory.

## The Main Rule

If it matters to a lead, client, proposal, audit or onboarding item, it should be visible in Mission Control.

Use Mission Control for:

- New leads and prospects
- Sales pipeline movement
- Notes and contact history
- Follow-up tasks
- Clinic Growth Score and audit status
- Proposals
- Won-client conversion
- Client onboarding
- Drive links and access/assets

Trello or ClickUp can still be used for wider delivery planning if the team decides to keep them. But anything linked to a lead, client or proposal should also be recorded in Mission Control so the account history is complete.

## Start Of Day

Each morning, open the dashboard and check:

- New leads
- Leads not contacted
- Hot or high-priority leads
- Follow-ups due today
- Overdue tasks
- Audits due or in progress
- Proposals needing follow-up
- Onboarding issues and missing access/assets

If something looks wrong on the dashboard, open the related record and fix the source record. Do not only make a note somewhere else.

## Adding A Lead

Use this flow when a lead comes in from phone, WhatsApp, email, referral, website form or a manual conversation.

Add as much of this as possible:

- Account or business name
- Contact name
- Email, phone or WhatsApp number
- Website
- Source
- Location
- Package interest
- Owner
- Next follow-up date

After saving:

1. Open the lead record and check it looks right.
2. Add a short internal note with useful context.
3. Set a follow-up if someone needs to act.
4. Move or create the opportunity in the correct pipeline stage.

Do not create empty records just to save something. If there is no name and no contact method, it is not useful yet.

## Duplicate Handling

Before creating a second record, check whether the lead already exists.

Mission Control checks strong matches like email, phone and website/domain. Account name and contact name matches should be reviewed by a person.

If a duplicate is flagged:

1. Open the duplicate review area.
2. Compare the account name, contact name, email, phone and website.
3. Mark it as merged, not duplicate or ignored.
4. Keep the useful source/history on the final record.

More detail is in `docs/duplicate-review.md`.

## Follow-Ups And Tasks

Every lead or client that needs action should have a task or follow-up.

Use tasks for:

- Calling back a lead
- Sending or chasing a proposal
- Following up after a guide download
- Completing audit or Growth Score work
- Requesting client access/assets
- Finishing onboarding steps
- Tracking a client issue

Each task should have:

- A clear title
- Owner
- Due date
- Priority
- Related lead/client/contact/proposal where possible
- Notes if context is needed

When the work is done, complete the task. If it becomes active again, reopen it instead of creating a confusing duplicate.

## Moving Pipeline Stages

The pipeline stage should show the real current sales position.

Basic flow:

1. New lead comes in.
2. Contact is needed or attempted.
3. Lead is spoken to or qualified.
4. Audit/Growth Score is requested or started.
5. Audit is completed.
6. Proposal is needed or sent.
7. Follow-up or negotiation happens.
8. Lead is marked Won, Lost, Nurture or Future Opportunity.

When moving to Lost, add the reason and objection if known. This will help improve sales, pricing and packages later.

When moving to Won, check that the client conversion and onboarding flow has been created correctly.

## Notes And Timeline

Use internal notes for useful context, for example:

- Call summary
- Buying intent
- Objections
- Package fit
- Decision maker details
- Promised follow-up
- Important email or WhatsApp context
- Internal risks or handover notes

Keep notes short, but useful. Another team member should be able to open the record and understand what happened without asking around.

Internal notes are for the ClinicGrower team only.

## Audits And Growth Score

Growth Score is part of the sales and audit process.

Use it to record:

- Overall score
- Category scores
- Gaps or weak areas
- Audit status
- Recommended next package
- Follow-up action after the audit

Keep the audit status updated so the team can see whether it is requested, assigned, started, completed, sent or due for follow-up.

If the audit is complete, the next action should usually be a proposal, dashboard/access follow-up or a clear sales follow-up.

## Proposals

Proposals should be created and tracked from Mission Control so they stay connected to the lead, contact, account and opportunity.

Use the proposal flow when:

- A lead is ready for pricing
- A Growth Score or audit has created a clear recommendation
- A package needs to be proposed
- A sent proposal needs chasing

Basic steps:

1. Create the proposal from the lead/deal/client record.
2. Pull in the available CRM, Growth Score and package data.
3. Edit any wording that needs to be personalized.
4. Check pricing and commercial fields.
5. Preview the proposal.
6. Generate the proposal link.
7. Send the link manually if automated email is not being used.
8. Log the proposal as sent in Mission Control.
9. Set the follow-up date.
10. Mark it accepted, won, lost or expired when the outcome is known.

The current MVP send flow is manual-send/link-based. More detail is in `docs/proposal-send-workflow.md`.

## Won Client Conversion

When a proposal or opportunity is won, convert it into a client account from the existing lead/deal. Do not create a disconnected client record unless there is a clear reason.

Before converting, check:

- Lead/contact details are correct.
- Proposal or agreed package is linked.
- Commercial basics are clear.
- Source and sales history should stay attached.

After converting:

1. Open the new client account.
2. Confirm the client status is separate from the old deal stage.
3. Confirm contact, source, notes, audit/Growth Score and proposal history are still linked.
4. Check that onboarding tasks/checklist items were created.
5. Assign owners and due dates where needed.

## Drive Links And Files

Drive links live on the lead/client record in the files/documents area.

Use the right field for each type of link:

- Main client folder
- Audit
- Proposal
- Contract/admin
- Onboarding
- Website/assets
- Reports
- Strategy/Looms
- Ads
- SEO/content

If a link is missing, inaccessible or wrong, update the record. Do not leave key folder links buried in Slack, email or old notes.

## Onboarding And Missing Access

New clients should have onboarding items visible on the client record.

The onboarding checklist should cover:

- Invoice
- GoCardless/payment setup
- Onboarding form
- Drive folder
- Website access
- GA4
- Google Search Console
- Google Tag Manager
- Google Ads
- Google Business Profile
- Meta
- Brand assets
- Treatment/pricing information
- Reporting setup
- Owner assignment
- First review

Missing access/assets should be marked as requested, received or not needed. Add a note if there is context, such as who was asked or what is blocking it.

## End Of Day

Before finishing the day:

- Clear or update overdue tasks.
- Make sure contact attempts are recorded.
- Set tomorrow's follow-ups.
- Check proposals that need chasing.
- Check won clients are not stuck without onboarding owners.
- Add notes where context would otherwise be lost.

## Team Handover

When handing over a lead or client, update Mission Control first.

Make sure the record shows:

- Current status
- Owner
- Next action
- Due date
- Relevant note
- Any missing access or risk

Then message the person if needed.

## What Not To Do

- Do not keep live lead status only in Scoro, WhatsApp, email or memory.
- Do not create duplicate leads because the first record is hard to find.
- Do not move pipeline stages just to make the board look cleaner.
- Do not mark a proposal sent unless the link/email has actually been sent.
- Do not convert to client until the won status and commercial basics are clear.
- Do not store client Drive links in random notes when there is a proper files/documents area.

## Go-Live Note

For phase one, Mission Control should be treated as the main internal CRM record.

Some integrations may still use fallback/manual steps while provider credentials and live webhooks are finalized. That is acceptable for MVP as long as the team records the activity and status inside Mission Control.

The success measure is simple: when someone opens a lead or client record, they should understand what happened, who owns it, what needs to happen next, and where the key proposal/files/onboarding items are.
